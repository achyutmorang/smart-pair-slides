\section{Contributions and Limitations}

\begin{frame}{Thesis Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Empirical comparison of uncertainty objective families in closed-loop scenario search.
    \item Explicit boundary on surprise objective infeasibility in the examined planner stack.
    \item Reproducible benchmark-first workflow for WOSAC-aligned experimentation.
    \item Practical direction toward risk-oriented, calibration-aware controller auditing.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Results depend on current planner/simulator implementation details.
    \item Feasibility constraints and perturbation families bound observable effects.
    \item Proxy metrics for failures and blind spots may miss nuanced behaviors.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.4em}
\textbf{Interpretive boundary:} findings are strong for this stack, not universal across all planners.
\end{frame}
