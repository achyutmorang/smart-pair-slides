\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

\begin{frame}{Conclusion}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Surprise is informative conceptually but not robust enough as primary optimization signal in this setting.
    \item Risk-centered objectives are more actionable for failure-oriented scenario search.
    \item Better calibration alone is insufficient when downstream discrimination remains weak.
    \item Baseline-first, reproducibility-first methodology yields stronger thesis contribution.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Future Work}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Improve planner-side signal fidelity before reattempting surprise-driven optimization.
    \item Expand WOSAC baseline reproduction and ablation depth.
    \item Test calibrated risk + gated surprise hybrids under stricter model-level controls.
    \item Validate transfer of findings across alternative planner architectures.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
