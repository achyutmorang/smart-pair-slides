\section{Results}

\begin{frame}{Key Empirical Findings}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Surprise-guided optimization shows a recurring feasibility gap in this planner stack.
    \item Despite nontrivial perturbations, surprise signals often collapse or fail realization criteria.
    \item Risk-centered objectives are more stable and decision-relevant for scenario auditing.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Calibration vs Control Utility}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Calibration-aware analysis improved confidence diagnostics.
    \item But improved calibration did \textbf{not} automatically improve thresholded control outcomes.
    \item Control performance remained limited when discrimination power was weak.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{block}{Interpretation}
Uncertainty quality and decision utility must be evaluated as separate axes.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
